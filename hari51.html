DO · DOES · DID

DO ➜ I / You / We / They → kebiasaan, pertanyaan, negatif (simple present)

DOES ➜ He / She / It → kebiasaan, pertanyaan, negatif + verb 1

DID ➜ semua subjek (I, You, He, She, We, They) → untuk masa lalu (simple past)

➕ Bentuk positif (penekanan): I do help / He does like / We did go

❓ Do you like? Does she work? Did they come? → pertanyaan
