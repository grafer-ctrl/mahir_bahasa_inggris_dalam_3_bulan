Bahasa Indonesia

Siang ini aku duduk sendirian di kamar sambil mendengarkan lagu favoritku. Aku membuka media sosial dan melihat banyak video lucu. Setelah itu aku makan biskuit dan minum teh manis. Sore hari aku membantu adik mencari bukunya yang hilang. Malamnya aku tidur sambil menyalakan kipas angin.

This afternoon, I sat alone in my room while listening to my favorite song. I opened social media and watched many funny videos. After that, I ate biscuits and drank sweet tea. In the evening, I helped my younger brother look for his lost book. At night, I slept with the fan on.
